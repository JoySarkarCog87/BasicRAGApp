Our mission

Large language models are excellent writers and unreliable librarians. Ask one a question outside its training data and it will still answer — fluently, confidently, and sometimes entirely wrong.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation fixes the librarian problem: fetch the real passage first, then let the model write from it. Our job is to make that retrieval step fast, auditable, and boring enough to put in production.

  • Ingest PDFs, text, and database records without custom scripts
  • Hybrid keyword and vector search for precise context
  • Every answer cites the chunks that produced it
